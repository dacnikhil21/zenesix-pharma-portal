Corporate Profile

Zenesix Pharmaceuticals is a healthcare-focused pharmaceutical marketing and distribution company committed to providing quality healthcare products. Under the stewardship of CH Bhanu Prakash, we manage a professional distribution network to supply trusted formulations to medical specialists and clinic groups across India.

We do not manufacture products; instead, we market and distribute quality formulations through WHO-GMP approved partner facilities. This collaboration allows us to provide reliable access to excellent, stable products with predictable, verified bio-absorption profiles across neurology, dermatology, urology, joint care, and pain management.
